**Project:** OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis  
**Dataset:** Netflix Titles Catalog

**Purpose:** Support data-informed content acquisition decisions.

## 1. Executive Summary

This analysis explores the Netflix titles catalog to understand its content mix, popular genres, release-year distribution, maturity classifications, country representation, and movie duration patterns.

The objective is to turn catalog data into practical recommendations for a content acquisition team.

## 3. Actionable Recommendations

1. **Genre strategy:** Shortlist promising titles in leading genres, while testing underserved genres with small pilot investments.
2. **Regional strategy:** Evaluate local-language acquisition opportunities in selected markets using audience demand and regional performance data.
3. **Format strategy:** Compare completion rates and retention across movie durations and TV show seasons before making format-specific investment decisions.

## 4. Limitations

This dataset describes a catalog snapshot. It does not provide complete streaming hours, customer engagement, subscriber retention, acquisition costs, licensing rights, or profitability.

Genre counts and country counts may depend on how multi-valued fields are classified. Release year is not the same as the year a title was added to Netflix.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis provides a structured view of the catalog and helps generate testable content acquisition hypotheses. Final greenlighting decisions should combine these findings with audience behavior, financial feasibility, and licensing information.
