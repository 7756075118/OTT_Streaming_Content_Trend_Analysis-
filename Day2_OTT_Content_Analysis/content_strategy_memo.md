# Content Strategy Memo: OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze OTT streaming content data to understand content distribution, popular genres, and release-year trends. The findings can help identify content categories that deserve attention when planning an OTT content library.

## 2. Key Findings

### A. Content Type Distribution

The dataset contains **6,234 titles** in total:

* **Movies:** 4,265 titles
* **TV Shows:** 1,969 titles

Movies account for approximately **68.4%** of the analyzed titles, while TV Shows account for approximately **31.6%**. This indicates that the dataset has a larger movie catalog than TV show catalog.

### B. Most Common Genres

The top genres in the dataset are:

1. International Movies — 1,927 titles
2. Dramas — 1,623 titles
3. Comedies — 1,113 titles
4. International TV Shows — 1,001 titles
5. Documentaries — 668 titles

International Movies and Dramas are the most frequently listed categories in the results. Comedy and international TV content also have a notable presence.

*Note: A title may belong to multiple genres, so genre counts can overlap.*

### C. Release-Year Trends

The release-year results show a general increase in titles from 2000 through 2018. The highest count in the provided results is **2018, with 1,063 titles**, followed by 2017 with 959 titles and 2016 with 830 titles.

The count decreases to 843 in 2019. The unusually low counts shown for 2020 (25 titles) may indicate incomplete or filtered data, so this result should be checked before drawing conclusions about content production in 2020.

## 3. Content Strategy Recommendations

1. **Maintain a strong movie catalog:** Movies make up approximately 68.4% of the analyzed titles. Continue evaluating movie demand while maintaining a suitable selection of TV Shows.
2. **Prioritize genre analysis:** International Movies and Dramas have the highest counts. Review audience engagement and viewing data to determine whether these categories should receive additional investment.
3. **Explore comedy and documentaries:** These genres have substantial representation and could be assessed for audience interest and content gaps.
4. **Review international content:** International Movies and International TV Shows appear frequently. Analyze country-level and audience-level performance to identify opportunities for regional and global content.
5. **Validate recent-year data:** Investigate why the 2020 count is much lower than the 2018 and 2019 counts before using the trend for planning.
6. **Use audience metrics for decisions:** Combine catalog counts with views, watch time, completion rates, and user ratings before deciding which genres or content types to expand.

## 4. Conclusion

The analysis shows that Movies form the majority of the dataset, while International Movies and Dramas are the most frequently listed genres. The release-year results rise substantially through 2018, but the low 2020 count needs validation. These findings provide an initial overview of the OTT catalog and can support content planning when combined with audience engagement and performance data.
